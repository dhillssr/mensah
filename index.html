About the Founder

Rooted in Athletics & Community

Dennis Mensah Jr., a professional and former Division I student-athlete, built the foundation on the discipline of athletics and a genuine heart for giving back — launching it as a personally funded grassroots movement to ensure no child is left without resources, encouragement, or a positive mentor.

"Community impact begins with consistency, compassion, and showing up for people who need support. The Mensah Foundation was built to create meaningful experiences for youth and families one event at a time."

— Dennis Mensah Jr.
